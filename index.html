💡 Cooking Tips & Tricks

Tip 1: Preparation is Key

Before you start cooking, make sure all your ingredients are washed, cut, and measured. This is called "mise en place." It helps you cook smoothly without rushing and prevents mistakes or burning your food.

Tip 2: Don't Rush the Heat

Cooking on high heat all the time does not make food cook faster — it often burns the outside while the inside remains raw. Be patient and use the right heat level for each ingredient.

Tip 3: Taste as You Go

Seasoning is not a one-time thing. Taste your food while cooking and adjust the salt, sugar, or spices little by little. It is easier to add more seasoning than to fix an over-salted dish.

Tip 4: Clean As You Cook

Keep your workspace clean while cooking. Wash utensils and wipe spills immediately. A clean kitchen makes cooking more enjoyable and less stressful.
